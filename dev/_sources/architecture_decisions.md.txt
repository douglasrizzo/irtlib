# Architecture Decision Records

Each Architecture Decision Record (ADR) states one decision that shapes irtlib's architecture: the
forces behind it, the rule, what follows from it and the options rejected. They are written for
contributors and method authors who want to know why the code is shaped as it is before they change
or extend it; the other pages describe what users observe and link the ADR for the reason. When a
decision changes, its ADR is edited in place, and git keeps the earlier text. The records read in
order, from the scope of the package to the contracts of its calibrators and estimators.
